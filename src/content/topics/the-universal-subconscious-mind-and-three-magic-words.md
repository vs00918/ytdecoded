# The Universal Subconscious Mind & Three Magic Words: U.S. Andersen on Mind-Form Causality, Mental Dietetics, and Non-Dual Sovereignty (1954)

## Executive Architectural Summary

In the lineage of twentieth-century American New Thought and metaphysical philosophy, Uell Stanley Andersen’s 1954 classic ***Three Magic Words: The Key to Power, Peace and Plenty*** stands as one of the most audacious, structurally coherent, and transformative syntheses of non-dual idealism, cognitive cybernetics, and practical psychology. Preceding and profoundly influencing later masters such as Dr. Joseph Murphy and Dr. Wayne Dyer, Andersen deconstructs the foundational error of human suffering: **the illusion of separation**.

Andersen posits that human consciousness does not possess a private, isolated subconscious mind. Rather, there exists only **One Universal Subconscious Mind**—an infinite, intelligent, omnipotent creative matrix that permeates every cubic centimeter of the universe, animating galaxies, guiding atomic orbits, and directing biological cell division. The individual conscious mind is an aperture, a focal lens via which this infinite sea of creative substance objectifies itself into three-dimensional physical form.

Human beings suffer from **"The Lock"**—a state of hypnotic limitation wherein the conscious intellect mistakenly identifies itself with the physical body and past memory records of pain, grief, and inadequacy. By dismantling this lock via a rigorous **Thirty-Day Mental Diet**, aligning the intellect with the **Intuitive Circuit**, commanding the **Law of Form** via unyielding **Faith**, and dissolving resentment via **Universal Love**, the individual awakens to the ultimate truth encapsulated in the three titular words: **"YOU ARE GOD."**

#### 1. The Genesis of the Lock
Andersen begins with a diagnosis of human limitation. Why does the average person experience chronic lack, somatic debility, and emotional turmoil despite harboring an inherent yearning for greatness?
* The barrier is **"The Lock"**—the subconscious accumulation of buried pain remembrances, early childhood conditioning, and cultural hypnotism.
* When a child experiences emotional injury, physical trauma, or rejection, the conscious mind withdraws in terror, burying the impression into the subconscious memory bank.
* The subconscious mind, being entirely deductive and impersonal, does not judge or discard these memories; it treats them as established instructions for reality.
* Consequently, the subconscious operates defensively, projecting an aura of fear, inadequacy, and vulnerability that continuously attracts identical situations in adult life.

#### 2. The Illusion of the Prison
Man mistakenly believes that his limitations are imposed from the outside—by economics, hereditary disease, social injustice, or bad luck. Andersen declares that **there are no external locks**.
* The external world is purely an effect; the lock is purely an internal habitual loop of thought.
* To pick the lock, one must withdraw energy from the sensory symptoms and recognize that the conscious mind alone holds the master key to reset the subconscious ledger.

### Chapter 2: Illusion (`58:53`) — Maya, Evil, and the Relativism of the Senses

#### 1. The Sensory Facade
Drawing upon ancient Eastern philosophy (Maya) and quantum insights, Andersen demonstrates that physical matter is not a solid, self-existing substance:
* What our five senses perceive as hard, static objects are actually rapid vortexes of vibrating energy governed by thought.
* The human eye registers only a microscopic sliver of the electromagnetic spectrum; the ear registers only a tiny band of sound frequencies.
* To accept sensory data as final, absolute reality is the ultimate delusion. The material world is a mirror, not the origin of reality.

#### 2. The Dissolution of "Evil"
Andersen presents a radical metaphysical position regarding the existence of evil:
* God—the Universal Subconscious Mind—is indivisible, infinite good, harmonious life, and limitless intelligence. There is no second creator, no cosmic devil, no eternal principle of darkness.
* What mankind calls "evil" is simply the misdirected creative power of thought. Evil is the shadow cast when the conscious mind projects images of fear, division, and greed into the creative matrix.
* Evil has no self-sustaining life; it exists only as long as human consciousness feeds it attention, fear, and belief. Remove belief and fear, and the illusion collapses into nothingness.

#### 1. The Monism of Consciousness
Andersen rejects the dualistic Freudian view that each human skull contains an isolated personal subconscious mind:
* There is only **One Subconscious Mind** in the universe, and we are all swimming inside it.
* Just as electricity is one universal force flowing through billions of light bulbs, heaters, and motors, the Universal Subconscious Mind is one continuous ocean of intelligent energy flowing through billions of human brains.
* Your thoughts do not merely stay inside your brain; they ripple out into the collective universal medium, setting cosmic machinery into motion.

#### 2. The Nature of the Creative Matrix
The Universal Subconscious Mind possesses three definitive characteristics:
1. **Omnipresence**: It is equally present in every point of time and space. Distance does not exist for it.
2. **Infinite Plasticity**: It takes any shape, form, or circumstance dictated by conscious conviction.
3. **Absolute Non-Resistance**: It never argues, debates, or corrects the conscious mind. If the conscious mind asserts "I am a failure," the Universal Subconscious accepts it as a command and arranges conditions to prove it true. If the conscious mind asserts "I am radiant health and victory," it executes that reality with equal fidelity.

### Chapter 4: Form (`02:55:58`) — The Law of Precipitation and Mental Blueprints

#### 1. Thought Precedes Substance
Everything that exists in the civilized world was once an invisible idea in human consciousness:
* The skyscraper, the bridge, the surgical instrument, the symphony, and the legal statute existed first in the mind before condensing into steel, stone, sound, and parchment.
* The physical universe operates by the exact same law: **Form is precipitated thought**.
* The conscious mind is the architect drawing the blueprint; the Universal Subconscious Mind is the master builder and the brick, mortar, and steel all at once.

#### 2. The Danger of Wavering Blueprints
Why do most people fail to manifest their ideals?
* They change the blueprint ten times a day.
* In the morning, they imagine wealth; by noon, they panic over a bill; by evening, they complain of economic hardship.
* The Universal Subconscious Mind begins building wealth, then tears down the construction to build poverty, producing chaotic, contradictory circumstances.
* Mastery requires **Blueprint Stability**: holding the mental image of the desired end with unshakeable continuity until the physical atoms coalesce into form.

#### 1. The Limits of the Conscious Intellect
The intellect is a wonderful computational tool for analyzing sensory measurements, but it is **incapable of original creation or omniscience**:
* When an individual faces a novel crisis or seeks a profound breakthrough, intellectual wrestling often results in anxiety and paralysis by analysis.
* The intellect knows only what it has been taught through sensory memory.

#### 2. The Intuitive Channel
Intuition is the direct voice of the Universal Subconscious Mind speaking into the quieted conscious chamber:
* It manifests as a flash of insight, an unshakeable inner conviction, a "hunch," or a sudden creative solution that emerges effortlessly.
* To activate intuition, the operator must silence the chatter of the intellect, release anxious striving, and enter a state of relaxed expectancy.
* Andersen instructs: Present the problem clearly to the Universal Mind, command the answer to appear, let go completely, and listen to the quiet impressions that arise during stillness or mundane activity.

### Chapter 6: Faith (`05:03:47`) — The Mathematical Mechanics of Creative Belief

#### 1. Faith as Scientific Law, Not Religious Sentimentality
Andersen strips the concept of faith of all theological vagueness:
* Faith is not blind hope, frantic begging, or desperate wishing.
* Faith is the **conscious acceptance of an unseen reality as already accomplished**.
* It is a psychological and metaphysical mechanism: the moment a mental condition is fully accepted as true by the conscious mind without mental reservation, the Universal Subconscious Mind is compelled by its own immutable nature to objectify it.

#### 2. The Paradox of Faith: Act as If
* If you wait for the physical money to arrive before feeling wealthy, or wait for the physical tumor to disappear before feeling whole, you are trapped in sensory worship.
* The subconscious cannot create from what is seen; it creates from **what is felt and believed in the unseen**.
* Faith demands that you align your thoughts, words, and feelings with the completed realization *prior* to any sensory validation. When you maintain that posture, the physical shadow must inevitably conform to the spiritual reality.

### Chapter 7: Attraction (`06:15:01`) — The Gravitational Coalescence of Ideas

#### 1. Mental Gravity
The Law of Attraction is the cosmic extension of physical gravitation into the mental ether:
* Just as massive celestial bodies exert gravitational pull on smaller objects, a strongly charged, concentrated mental concept exerts a magnetic pull upon corresponding people, circumstances, resources, and events.
* You do not attract what you *want*; you attract what you **are**—what you unconsciously dwell upon, expect, and believe to be true.

#### 2. The Center of Consciousness
Every individual lives at the center of their own self-created energetic vortex:
* If your internal core is magnetized with self-pity, grievance, and fear, you become a magnet for betrayal, financial loss, and disease.
* If you consciously re-magnetize your core with peace, confidence, integrity, and abundance, identical vibrational patterns in the Universal Mind are drawn into your immediate physical orbit with mathematical certainty.

* Fear is the inversion of faith—it is faith in evil, faith in sickness, faith in failure.

### Chapter 9: Success (`08:05:01`) — Directing the Subconscious for Prosperity and Service

#### 1. The True Definition of Success
Success is not the ruthless accumulation of material hoardings via predation or frantic competition:
* Real success is the **full outward realization and expression of your inner spiritual divinity**.
* It is living purposefully, creatively, and abundantly, rendering tremendous service to the whole while receiving the natural overflow of the universe's limitless wealth.

#### 2. The Elimination of the Scarcity Illusion
* Scarcity exists only in human systems, never in nature or the Universal Mind.
* The universe is teeming with infinite abundance, endless energy, and inexhaustible opportunity.
* To achieve success, one must completely eradicate the poverty consciousness that believes one person's gain requires another's loss.
* In the Universal Subconscious Mind, wealth is created from within. Connect your purpose to the upliftment and service of humanity, and wealth becomes the automatic mathematical byproduct of your aligned consciousness.

* Science has proven that energy cannot be created and cannot be destroyed; it can only change form.

* The physical body is merely an overcoat worn by the soul for a brief journey through three-dimensional spacetime.
* When the overcoat wears out or is cast aside, the conscious identity does not perish; it steps out of the sensory vehicle into a higher, freer dimension of conscious expression.

#### 2. Living in Eternity Now
* Immortality is not something that happens after a physical funeral; **you are immortal right now**.
* Birth was not your beginning, and physical death will not be your end.
* When an individual realizes their true identity as birthless, deathless spirit, the last remnant of fear evaporates from their being.

```
       1. TOTAL VIGILANCE:
          * For 30 consecutive days, you must not harbor or indulge in a single negative,

            immediately neutralize it by declaring: "There is no truth in this. It has no
            power over me. I accept only good, truth, and infinite divine life."

       3. THE RESTART RULE:
          * If you surrender to negative ruminating, indulge in an emotional rage, or harbor
            morbid fears for an extended period, you must reset the count to Day 1.

       4. SENSORY DETOXIFICATION:
          * Cease reading sensationalized crime reports, morbid news broadcasts, and engaging
            in cynical gossip that feeds destructive imagery into your subconscious matrix.
       =========================================================================
```
