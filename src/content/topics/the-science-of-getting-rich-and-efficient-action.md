# The Architecture of the Creative Plane & Efficient Action: Wallace D. Wattles' *The Science of Getting Rich* (1910)

> *"There is a science of getting rich, and it is an exact science, like algebra or arithmetic. There are certain laws which govern the process of acquiring riches; once these laws are learned and obeyed by any man, he will get rich with mathematical certainty."*  
> — **Wallace D. Wattles, *The Science of Getting Rich* (1910)**

---

## Executive Architectural Summary

Published in 1910 in Holyoke, Massachusetts by Elizabeth Towne's *Nautilus* press, **_The Science of Getting Rich_** by **Wallace Delois Wattles** (1860–1911) represents the foundational operational manual of 20th-century wealth psychology. 

Where contemporary treatises treated wealth either as a moral hazard (the Puritan work ethic) or as a zero-sum Darwinian struggle (Robber Baron capitalism), Wattles performed a radical philosophical synthesis: **He reframed the acquisition of wealth as a moral duty, an exact deterministic science, and a positive-sum creative process rooted in monistic cosmology.**

Wattles’ core thesis dismantled the fatalistic myth that financial success is dictated by environment, innate genius, capital hoarding, or luck. By studying successful individuals across diverse geographies and intellects, Wattles discovered the invariant cause: **People get rich because they do things in a "Certain Way."**

The impact of this 1910 text reverberated throughout the 20th and 21st centuries:
* It served as the direct philosophical blueprint for **Earl Nightingale’s *The Strangest Secret* (1956)** and Napoleon Hill's later syntheses.
* It was famously cited by producer Rhonda Byrne as the spark that ignited the global phenomenon *The Secret* (2006).
* Crucially, unlike modern caricatures that reduce mental manifestation to passive daydreaming, Wattles established an uncompromising doctrine of **Efficient Action in the Living Present**: *Thought impels wealth toward you, but personal action receives it.*

## 1. The Right to Be Rich: Moral Duty vs Poverty Asceticism

Wattles opens his treatise with an uncompromising attack on the romanticization of poverty:

> *"Whatever may be said in praise of poverty, the fact remains that it is not possible to live a really complete or successful life unless one is rich."*

### The Tripartite Nature of Human Unfolding
A human being lives across three co-equal dimensions: **Body, Mind, and Soul**. None is superior or more sacred than the others:
* **The Body**: Requires wholesome food, comfortable clothing, warm shelter, freedom from exhausting toil, and physical recreation.
* **The Mind**: Requires books, intellectual leisure, travel, observation, and artistic contemplation.
* **The Soul**: Requires love—and love is denied its highest expression where poverty prevents a person from bestowing gifts and benefits upon those they cherish.

To be fully alive in all three realms requires physical things; and because society is organized around commerce, **a person must possess money in abundance to command the instruments of full biological, mental, and spiritual development.** 

Wattles sweeps away theological guilt: Wanting to be rich is simply the desire for a richer, fuller, and more abundant life—an entirely noble and divine aspiration.

## 2. The Science of Getting Rich: Disproving Environmental Myths

Wattles proves that getting rich is not governed by external constraints through a series of logical eliminations:

1. **It is NOT a matter of Environment**: If it were, all people in a specific town or valley would be millionaires, while those in adjacent districts would be paupers. Yet in every city, side by side in the same climate, same laws, and same industry, one man becomes wealthy while another fails.
2. **It is NOT a matter of Superior Talent**: Many people of towering intellectual ability remain hopelessly poor, while individuals of mediocre intellect amass substantial fortunes.
3. **It is NOT a matter of Saving or Thrift**: Many extreme misers die in poverty, while free spenders frequently accumulate great wealth.
4. **It is NOT a matter of Doing a Unique Business**: Two men in the exact same commercial trade on the same street often experience opposite results—one thrives while the other goes bankrupt.

**The Invariant Law**: *Getting rich is the deterministic result of doing things in a "Certain Way."* Anyone who follows these rules—regardless of location, education, or background—will get rich with mathematical precision.

## 3. The Monistic Foundation: The Formless Thinking Substance

Wattles roots his operational methodology in classical philosophical monism—the doctrine found in Hindu philosophy and in the systems of Descartes, Spinoza, Leibniz, Schopenhauer, Hegel, and Ralph Waldo Emerson: **One is All, and All is One.**

### The Core Metaphysical Formula
Wattles condenses this cosmology into three interlocking propositions:
$$\begin{aligned}
\mathbf{Proposition\ 1:} & \quad \text{There is a thinking stuff from which all things are made.} \\
\mathbf{Proposition\ 2:} & \quad \text{A thought of form in this substance produces the form.} \\
\mathbf{Proposition\ 3:} & \quad \text{Man can impress his thought upon Formless Substance to create what he envisions.}
\end{aligned}$$

### The Law of Increasing Life
The Formless Substance is not dead, mechanical matter; it is **Living Intelligence**. The inherent nature of all living intelligence is the drive for **continuous expansion, fuller expression, and more life**:
* A single seed dropped into fertile soil multiplies itself into hundreds of seeds.
* Every thought generated compels the mind to think another thought.
* The universe is continually moving toward more life and greater complexity.

Therefore, when a human being seeks to create wealth to enlarge life for themselves and others, **the entire momentum of the universe is aligned with their success.**

* **The Creative Truth**: Raw materials can never be exhausted. If the earth runs short of gold, lumber, or steel, the Formless Substance will create more. You never need to fight for what is already created; you create directly from the unformed supply.

## 5. Gratitude: The Master Harmonic Transducer

To impress your mental vision upon Formless Substance, your mind must be held in close, continuous alignment with the Source. Wattles establishes that this alignment is achieved through a single operational state: **Profound, Unshakable Gratitude.**

### The Three Stages of Gratitude Alignment
1. **Belief in the Source**: Recognizing that there is One Intelligent Substance behind all phenomena.
2. **Belief in Abundance**: Believing that this Substance freely gives you everything you desire.
3. **Continuous Thankfulness**: Relating yourself to this Substance by daily, heartfelt thanksgiving.

### The Antidote to Cynicism and Decay
The moment you allow your mind to dwell on dissatisfaction, corrupt politicians, ruthless monopolies, or unfair conditions, you polarize your consciousness with scarcity.
* What you habitually dwell upon, you draw into your experience.
* Dwelling on the sordid, inferior, and unjust reproduces those identical qualities in your mental image.
* **Gratitude acts as a cognitive filter**: It keeps your attention fixed exclusively on the highest, most advancing elements of reality, ensuring that your mental image remains pristine.

## 6. Thinking in the Certain Way: Precision of Vision & Will

To transmit a desire to the Formless Substance, you must not indulge in vague generalities like *"I want to do good"* or *"I want plenty of money."*

### I. The Blueprint Invariant
A builder cannot construct a mansion from a vague wish for shelter; an architect must draft every room, dimension, and doorway:
* Define the exact life you intend to lead, the home you will inhabit, the enterprise you will direct, and the exact tools you will wield.
* Spend your leisure hours contemplating this vision until it is etched into consciousness like a photograph.

### II. The Proper Use of the Will
Wattles issues a severe warning against the metaphysical misuse of willpower:
* **The Error of Hypnotic Coercion**: Never attempt to project mental willpower outward to compel other people to buy from you, obey you, or surrender their property. To mentally coerce another is as criminal as physical extortion.
* **The Internal Locus of the Will**: Willpower is meant to be applied exclusively **to your own mind**. You use your will to keep your attention pinned to your Vision, to sustain your Faith and Purpose, and to violently expel doubt, pessimism, and competitive jealousy from your consciousness.

## 7. Acting in the Certain Way: Efficient Action & The Living Present

This is where Wattles separates scientific achievement from empty occult fantasy. Many metaphysical seekers meet financial disaster because they believe visualization alone will materialize checks in their mailbox.

> *"By thought, the thing you want is brought to you; by action you receive it."*

### I. Grounding in the Living Present
You cannot act in the past; that is gone. You cannot act in the future; that is not yet here. **You can only act NOW.**
* Do not wait for a change of environment or career before you begin acting.
* Use your present job, your present desk, and your present relationships as the staging ground to demonstrate advancing capability.

### II. Overfilling Your Present Place
Evolutionary biology proves that an animal species only evolves when an organism possesses **an excess of life** beyond what can be expressed in its current physical form.
* The employee who merely does the bare minimum to keep their job is "smaller than their place"—a dead weight dragging down commerce.
* To advance, you must be **larger than your present place**. You must do every single task assigned to you with absolute excellence, while radiating the conscious purpose to achieve greatness.
* An individual who consistently overfills their present place cannot be held down; social and commercial forces inevitably open a wider sphere of authority.

### III. The Invariant of Efficient Action
Success is not a matter of volume; it is a matter of **efficiency**:
* Every act is in itself either a **Success** or a **Failure**.
* Every inefficient act is a failure; if you spend your day performing inefficient actions, your life is a mathematical failure.
* The secret is not to perform a million frantic tasks, but to **put the whole power of your purpose into every single act you execute**, no matter how small. 
* Compounding efficient acts creates an unstoppable tidal wave of kinetic momentum.

## 8. The Impression of Increase & Use-Value Asymmetry

To succeed in any enterprise involving human relations, Wattles provides two non-negotiable commercial laws:

### I. The Use-Value Asymmetry
> *"You must give every man more in use value than you take from him in cash value."*
* **Cash Value**: The nominal monetary price charged for a good or service.
* **Use Value**: The utility, empowerment, education, and advancement the good confers upon the user.
* **The Rule**: If you sell a business system, a piece of software, or an education for \$100, ensure that the utility it delivers generates \$1,000 of efficiency, health, or happiness for the buyer. In this way, every single transaction increases the net wealth of the world.

### II. The Impression of Increase
Every human being is driven by the innate cosmic desire for *Increase*—more life, more power, more beauty, more joy. People instinctively flock to leaders, doctors, teachers, merchants, and friends who radiate advancement:
* When you speak to an employee, a client, or a stranger, let your bearing, tone, and presence convey the quiet, unshakable conviction: *"I am advancing, and in associating with me, you will advance too."*
* Never boast, preach, or act superior; simply radiate the calm certainty of continuous growth.
