# The Architecture of Objective Definiteness & Practical Teleology: RHJ's *It Works*

> *"The author sent the manuscript of this book for criticism to a friend who returned it with the notation: 'It works.' This judgment, born of actual experience, has been reaffirmed by thousands of men and women who have used the simple plan it presents."*  
> — **Roy Herbert Jarrett (RHJ), *It Works* (1926)**

---

## Executive Architectural Summary

Published in 1926 under the pseudonym **RHJ** by Chicago advertising executive and salesman **Roy Herbert Jarrett**, *It Works: The Famous Little Red Book That Makes Your Dreams Come True* represents the most radical compression of goal teleology and subconscious programming in twentieth-century literature.

Where monumental philosophical treatises such as Haanel’s *Master Key System* (1912) and *The Kybalion* (1908) required hundreds of pages of dense metaphysical analysis, Jarrett performed a ruthless editorial excision: **He stripped away all occult terminology, religious dogma, and psychological theorizing, reducing the entire science of human achievement to a single, 15-minute operational blueprint.**

Jarrett's foundational insight is as blunt as it is universally verified: **98% of human beings fail to achieve what they want because they do not know what they want.** They possess vague, conflicting, wandering daydreams—"more money," "a better life," "happiness"—which provide zero steering coordinates for the human nervous system.

By translating subconscious dynamics into three uncompromising rules of daily behavior, *It Works* created the prototype for modern goal setting, directly inspiring Earl Nightingale's iconic 1956 recording *The Strangest Secret* and prefiguring modern neuroscientific findings on the **Reticular Activating System (RAS)**.

## The Core Pathology: The Curse of Ambiguity

The overwhelming majority of human beings wander through life in a permanent trance of unfulfilled longing. When questioned about their life objectives, they produce generic platitudes:
* *"I want financial freedom."*
* *"I want to be happy."*
* *"I want a good relationship."*
* *"I want to travel."*

RHJ diagnoses this as fatal cognitive negligence. The inner creative faculty of the human psyche cannot act upon generalities:
1. **The Navigational Void:** Telling your subconscious mind "I want money" is equivalent to entering a taxicab and telling the driver "Take me somewhere nice." The driver cannot move until an exact street address is provided.
2. **The Test of Earnestness:** If an individual cannot articulate the exact make, model, year, and color of the car they desire, or the exact dollar figure and deadline of the revenue they seek, **they are not in earnest**. Vagueness is the unmistakable signature of passive wishful thinking.

### Rule 1: The Rhythmic 3x Daily Review
> *"Read the list of what you want three times each day: morning, noon, and night."*

* **The Neurobiology of State Transitions:** The morning and night reviews exploit hypnagogic and hypnopompic brainwave states (alpha and theta transitions), where the critical conscious filter is lowered and the subconscious disc is exceptionally malleable.
* **The Noon Reset:** The midday review functions as a cognitive circuit-breaker, rescuing the operator from the chaotic, reactive demands of the workday and re-anchoring attention in primary teleological goals.

---

### Rule 2: Continuous Daytime Recall
> *"Think of what you want as often as possible."*

* **Dominating the Default Mode Network (DMN):** When the human brain is not engaged in focused tasks, the DMN defaults to anxious rumination, past regrets, and threat detection. Rule 2 deliberately reprograms the idle state of the brain.
* **Tuning the Reticular Activating System (RAS):** By holding the desired objective in the forefront of consciousness, the brain’s sensory filters are sensitized. Resources, people, books, conversations, and opportunities that were previously invisible suddenly leap out of the ambient noise.

---

### Rule 3: The Iron Law of Social Secrecy
> *"Do not talk to anyone about your plan except to the Great Power within you which will unfold to your objective mind the method of accomplishment."*

This is the single most violated rule in personal achievement, and the primary cause of aborted dreams. RHJ insists upon absolute, unbroken silence for two profound reasons:

```
                    THE PATHOLOGY OF PREMATURE DISCLOSURE
  Internal Ambition ──► [ PREMATURE DISCLOSURE ] ──► Ambient Cynicism & Doubt
                                │
                                ▼
                   1. Social Dopamine Prematurely Vented
                   2. Impressionable Mind Infected by Skeptics
                   3. Loss of Psychic Pressure $\to$ Failure
```

1. **The Vulnerability to Suggestion:** The objective conscious mind is fragile and highly susceptible to outside suggestion. If you tell a skeptical friend, coworker, or relative that you intend to earn \$100,000 or build an extraordinary enterprise, their polite smiles, arched eyebrows, or overt cynicism instantly contaminate your subconscious with doubt.
2. **The Dissipation of Kinetic Drive:** Modern neuroscience demonstrates that announcing a goal publicly generates a premature burst of social dopamine—the brain tricks itself into feeling it has already achieved the status, thereby drastically reducing the kinetic motivation required to do the actual grinding work. **Keep your mouth shut until the objective is materialized.**

RHJ instructs the operator to write down everything they desire without false modesty or self-censorship, but demands uncompromising granularity:

## Deconstructing the "HOW": The Grain of Corn Metaphor

The greatest obstacle confronting analytical thinkers is the obsession with figuring out the exact step-by-step causal mechanism *before* setting the goal: *"How on earth could I possibly earn that much money? Where would it come from? Who would give it to me?"*

RHJ dismisses this as an absurd category error:

> *"There is no need to analyze how this Power within you is going to accomplish your desires. Such a procedure is as unnecessary as trying to figure out why a grain of corn placed in fertile soil shoots up a green stalk, blossoms and produces an ear of corn containing hundreds of grains, each capable of doing what the one grain did."*

```
                     THE CORN SEED INCUBATION CYCLE
 ┌──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
 │ 1. THE SEED       : The written, unambiguous desire (The "WHAT").        │
 │ 2. THE SOIL       : The subconscious creative matrix (Omnipotent Power). │
 │ 3. THE NURTURING  : 3x daily rhythmic review and grateful recall.        │
 │ 4. THE FATAL ERROR: Digging up the seed daily to inspect the roots.      │
 │ 5. THE HARVEST    : Emergence of ideas, hunches, doors opening (The HOW).│
 └──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

The conscious intellect is an **evaluator**, not the **generator**. The conscious mind chooses the seed; the subconscious soil germinates it. Demanding to know the entire causal pathway in advance paralyzes action and aborts the seed.

## The Ancient Enemy: Overcoming Discredit & Coincidence

RHJ warns that the moment an individual experiences their first concrete demonstration of the law, a lethal psychological defense mechanism will activate:

> *"A natural and ancient enemy will no doubt appear when you get your first taste of accomplishment. This enemy is discredit, in form of such thoughts as: 'It can't be possible; it just happened to be. What a remarkable coincidence!'"*

### The Anatomy of Discredit
* **The Ego’s Resistance to Magic:** The cynical conscious intellect hates to admit that its narrow analytical logic was bypassed. It desperately seeks to preserve its illusion of mundane control by labeling miraculous synchronicity as "mere luck" or "random chance."
* **The Lethal Danger:** The moment you label a demonstration a "coincidence," you sever the feedback loop of faith. You tell the subconscious that its work was irrelevant, thereby shutting down the generative engine.

### The Antidote: Aggressive Gratitude
Whenever an item on your list materializes—no matter how mundane the apparent physical mechanism:
1. **Never say "It just happened."**
2. **Assert immediate vocal credit** to the Omnipotent Power within.
3. **Flood your nervous system with genuine gratitude and joy.** Sincere thanksgiving cements the neural pattern, validates the law, and accelerates the manifestation of the next desire.

## The Ecological Cautionary Clause: Systemic Consequences

RHJ issues a profound warning that elevates *It Works* from a simplistic greed manual into a mature philosophy of life:

> *"Caution. It is possible to want and obtain that which will make you miserable; that which will wreck the happiness of others; that which will cause sickness and death; that which will rob you of eternal life. You can have what you want, but you must take all that goes with it."*

   Direct Benefit           Collateral Impact           Systemic Harmony

  authentic purpose?       integrity, or relationships? alongside myself?

* **The Inviolable Law of Package Deals:** Reality does not sell attributes à la carte. If you demand a ruthless corporate promotion without ecological balance, you receive the promotion along with ulcers, insomnia, family disintegration, and acute cynicism.
* **The Ethical Filter:** Before cementing any desire onto your permanent list, ask: *Am I willing to accept everything that naturally accompanies this reality? Does this contribute to the greatest good of myself and my fellow human beings?*

## The Stepping-Stone Heuristic: Building Operational Faith

RHJ recognizes that faith cannot be manufactured out of thin air by an act of will. A novice cannot command the healing of advanced pathology or the transformation of a multi-million-dollar empire on day one without drowning in skepticism.

Therefore, he establishes **The Stepping-Stone Heuristic**:

```
                       THE LADDER OF DEMONSTRATION
 ┌──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
 │ LEVEL 3: TRANSCENDENTAL & ALTRUISTIC (Habits, Healing, Service, Legacy)  │
 ├──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
 │ LEVEL 2: EXPANDED HORIZONS (Career pivots, significant wealth, homes)    │
 ├──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
 │ LEVEL 1: FAMILIAR & MEASURABLE (Specific checks, clothes, sales records) │
 └──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

1. **Begin with the Familiar:** Start your first list with tangible, measurable, immediate things with which your mind is already comfortable: an extra \$500, a specific meeting, a new suit, an overdue account settled.
2. **Generate Empirical Proof:** When these lesser items materialize with eerie precision, your conscious mind receives empirical data that it cannot argue against.
3. **Ascend to the Summit:** With your faith grounded in personal verification, you can fearlessly tackle life-altering habits, physical health, and massive humanitarian contributions.

## Active Recall & Deep Comprehension Drills

### Drill 1: The Three Rules Under Pressure
* **Question:** You are facing an intense week where you feel overwhelmed and have no time. What are the non-negotiable minimum requirements of the RHJ system?
* **Answer:** Pull out the physical list exactly three times—upon waking, at noon, and before sleep. Read every word slowly. Throughout the day, whenever anxiety strikes, replace the anxious thought with the mental image of the top item on your list. Never mention your struggles or goals to coworkers or skeptics.

### Drill 2: Diagnosing Discredit
* **Question:** You write down a demand for \$2,500. Three days later, an old client calls unexpectedly and hires you for a \$2,500 project. Your intellect immediately says: *"Well, they were probably going to call anyway."* How do you respond?
* **Answer:** Identify this thought as the "ancient enemy" of Discredit attempting to sabotage your subconscious momentum. Immediately refuse the thought. Declare conscious credit and heartfelt gratitude to your inner power, record the date of accomplishment on the list, strike through the item, and promote the next goal.

### Drill 3: The Definiteness Audit
* **Question:** A student writes on their list: *"I want to be a successful writer."* Audit this entry according to RHJ’s standards.
* **Answer:** The entry fails completely due to fatal ambiguity. A properly formatted entry must state: *"Publishing my 65,000-word nonfiction manuscript on cognitive cybernetics with an established trade publisher by September 2027, receiving a minimum advance of \$25,000, and selling 10,000 copies in year one."*
