### 1. IRDAI Master Framework: Insurance Intermediaries Governance & Investment Limits
* **Priority:** P1 — Critical / Deep
* **Category:** INSURANCE_SECTOR
* **Institution:** IRDAI
* **Event Date:** 2026-08-05
* **Revision Effort:** ~8 min
* **Status:** NOTIFIED

#### What Happened
* The Insurance Regulatory and Development Authority of India (IRDAI) overhauled the regulatory and investment framework governing insurance intermediaries, establishing perpetual registrations with annual turnover-linked fees, strict training mandates, and expanded investment headroom for insurers in infrastructure and private equity.

#### Must Memorize
* **Perpetual Registration & Annual Fee Formula:**
  * Certificate of registration, once issued to intermediaries (corporate agents, brokers, marketing firms, web aggregators, CPSC-SPVs), remains **perpetually valid** subject to payment of an annual non-refundable fee.
  * **Annual Fee Formula:** \(\text{Annual Fee} = \max(₹10,000,\ 0.04\% \times \text{Commission \& receipts from insurers in preceding FY})\)
* **Training Mandate for Insurance Marketing Firms (IMFs):**
  * Principal officers and insurance salespersons must complete at least **25 hours of training once every three years** through an IRDAI-recognised examination body.
* **Insurer Investment Limits & Exposure Ceilings:**
  * **Infrastructure SPVs:** Insurers permitted to invest up to **20%** in operational infrastructure special purpose vehicles (SPVs) rated **AA or above**.
  * **Private Companies, AIFs & VCFs:** Combined investment limit in private limited companies, Alternative Investment Funds (AIFs), and Venture Capital Funds (VCFs) is capped at **3% of investment assets for life insurers** and **5% for general insurers**.
  * **Promoter Group Exposure:** Overall exposure to promoter groups is capped at **5% of total investment assets**.
  * **Repo & G-Sec Lending:** Repo and government securities lending transactions are capped at **25% of available securities or ₹10,000 crore**, whichever is lower.

#### Know / Understand
* Shifts the regulatory model from repetitive periodic license renewals to continuous compliance backed by an annual turnover-linked fee and mandatory ongoing professional training (25 hrs/3 yrs).

#### Exam Focus
* Intermediary annual fee formula: higher of ₹10,000 or 0.04% of receipts.
* Training requirement for marketing firms: 25 hours every 3 years.
* Insurer investment limits in Private Cos/AIFs/VCFs: 3% (Life) vs 5% (General).
* Infrastructure SPV cap: 20% (for AA-rated or above).
* Promoter group ceiling: 5% of investment assets.

### 2. Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, 2023: Statutory Penalties & Regulatory Framework
* **Priority:** P1 — Critical / Memorize
* **Category:** BANKING_REGULATION
* **Institution:** Ministry of Electronics & IT (MeitY) / DPBI
* **Event Date:** 2026-08-04
* **Revision Effort:** ~7 min
* **Status:** NOTIFIED

#### What Happened
* Comprehensive enforcement guidelines under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDPA), establishing statutory compliance standards and high-quantum monetary penalties for data fiduciaries across banking, fintech, and commercial sectors.

#### Must Memorize
* **Statutory Penalty Thresholds:**
  * **Security Failure (Data Breach):** Failure to take reasonable security safeguards to prevent personal data breaches attracts a maximum penalty of up to **₹250 crore**.
  * **Breach Notification Failure:** Failure to notify the Data Protection Board of India (DPBI) and affected individuals in the event of a breach attracts a penalty of up to **₹200 crore**.
  * **Non-Fulfillment of Children's Data Duties:** Maximum penalty of up to **₹200 crore**.
  * **General Non-Compliance:** Penalty of up to **₹50 crore** for other residual violations.
* **Scope & Applicability:**
  * Applies to all digital personal data processed within India, including employee data, customer KYC data, and digital transaction trails across both private and public entities.

#### Know / Understand
* Imposes massive balance-sheet accountability on banks and fintechs handling customer financial and identity data, making cybersecurity safeguards a top board-level governance requirement.

#### Exam Focus
* Maximum penalty for failure to prevent personal data breach: ₹250 crore.
* Maximum penalty for failure to notify data breach: ₹200 crore.
* Maximum penalty for child data violation: ₹200 crore.
