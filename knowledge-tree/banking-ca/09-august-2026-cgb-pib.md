### 3. KCC-MISS Assessment: ₹1 Investment Generates ₹2.30 Net Agricultural Value
* **Priority:** P1 — Critical / Deep | **Revision Effort:** ~7 min | **Category:** Agricultural Finance & Priority Lending
* **Source:** CGB Mentors (PIB 1st–10th August 2026) | **Status:** THIRD-PARTY EVALUATION RELEASED

**What Happened**
* The Ministry of Finance released the independent third-party evaluation study of the *Kisan Credit Card – Modified Interest Subvention Scheme (KCC-MISS)* conducted by the **Institute for Social and Economic Change (ISEC), Bengaluru**.

**Must Memorize (Core Findings & KCC Enhancements)**
* **Economic Multiplier**: Every **₹1 invested under KCC-MISS generated ₹2.30 net value addition** in agriculture and allied sectors.
* **Cumulative Fiscal Outlay**: **₹1.87 lakh crore** disbursed under MISS since inception through FY 2024–25.
* **Collateral-Free Loan Limit**: Enhanced from **₹1.6 lakh to ₹2 lakh** (effective **1 January 2025**).
* **Digital Agricultural Stack**:
  * **Kisan Rin Portal (KRP)**: Integrated digital portal for real-time tracking of agricultural credit delivery.
  * **Jan Samarth Portal**: Single-window digital platform for credit-linked government subsidy schemes.
  * **e-KCC**: End-to-end digital processing and sanction of Kisan Credit Cards.
  * **KRISHIKA**: AI-driven technology platform for precision agricultural credit underwriting.

**Know / Understand**
* KCC-MISS provides 1.5% interest subvention to lending institutions and 3% Prompt Repayment Incentive (PRI) to farmers, resulting in an effective crop loan rate of 4% p.a. for loans up to ₹3 lakh.

### 4. Pradhan Mantri Viksit Bharat Rojgar Yojana (PM-VBRY): Two-Part Incentive Structure
* **Priority:** P1 — Critical / Memorize | **Revision Effort:** ~8 min | **Category:** Employment & Social Security
* **Source:** CGB Mentors (PIB 1st–10th August 2026) | **Status:** IMPLEMENTED / 1-YEAR MILESTONE

**What Happened**
* The Ministry of Labour & Employment reviewed the first-year implementation of the *Pradhan Mantri Viksit Bharat Rojgar Yojana (PM-VBRY)*, showing over **72 lakh first-time formal workers** enrolled.

**Must Memorize (Incentive Parameters)**
* **Implementation Window**: **1 August 2025 to 31 July 2027** (2-year tenure).
* **Total Outlay**: **₹99,446 crore**.
* **Target**: Over **3.5 crore employment opportunities**, including **1.92 crore first-time formal employees**.
* **Part A — First-Time Employees**:
  * Eligibility: First-time formal workers earning **up to ₹1 lakh/month**.
  * Incentive: **One month's EPF wage, capped at ₹15,000**.
  * Disbursement: Paid in **2 equal instalments** (1st instalment after **6 months** of service; 2nd instalment after **12 months** of continuous service + completion of a mandatory financial literacy module).
  * Savings Mandate: A portion of the incentive is compulsorily invested in a designated savings instrument.
* **Part B — Employers**:
  * Eligibility: For hiring additional employees earning up to ₹1 lakh/month.
  * Incentive: **Up to ₹3,000/month per eligible additional employee for 2 years** (retained for at least 6 months).
  * **Manufacturing Extension**: For the manufacturing sector, incentives are extended into the **3rd and 4th years**.
* **One-Year Performance (August 2026)**: **72+ lakh** first-time employees added to formal payrolls; **~30% are women**.
* **Digital Authentication**: Mandatory UAN Face Authentication on the **UMANG App** and direct DBT to Aadhaar-linked accounts.

### 5. Mobile Phone Manufacturing Scheme (MPMS) & Semicon 2.0 (₹1.90 Lakh Crore Combined Push)
* **Priority:** P1 — Critical / Deep | **Revision Effort:** ~7 min | **Category:** Electronics Manufacturing & Industrial Policy
* **Source:** CGB Mentors (PIB 1st–10th August 2026) | **Status:** APPROVED BY UNION CABINET

**What Happened**
* The Union Cabinet approved two massive electronics manufacturing frameworks: the *Mobile Phone Manufacturing Scheme (MPMS)* with **₹62,500 crore** outlay and *Semicon 2.0* with **₹1,27,500 crore** outlay.

**Must Memorize (MPMS & Semicon 2.0 Specifications)**
* **MPMS (Mobile Phone Manufacturing Scheme)**:
  * Outlay & Tenure: **₹62,500 crore** over 5 years (**FY 2026–27 to FY 2030–31**).
  * Incentive Slabs: **2.25% to 5%** on incremental sales of manufactured mobile phones.
  * Additional Component Incentive: **Up to 1.5% additional** for domestic component/sub-assembly sourcing.
  * Indian Brand Incentive: **Additional 3%** for Indian-owned brands investing in design, R&D, and IP.
  * Macro Context: Replaced PLI-LSEM which expired on **31 March 2026**. Smartphones became India's **#1 exported product category in 2025** (surpassing diesel and cut diamonds). India is the **2nd-largest mobile manufacturer globally by volume** with **99.2%** domestic manufacturing share.
* **Semicon 2.0**:
  * Total Outlay: **₹1,27,500 crore**.
  * 6 Core Pillars: 1. Design (105 startups already developing chips), 2. Machines & Materials, 3. Fabs (Silicon & Compound), 4. ATMP/OSAT, 5. Advanced R&D Nodes, 6. Talent Development.
