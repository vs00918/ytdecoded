### 1. SEBI Revised Commodity ETF Framework: Dynamic Price Bands & Pre-Open Auction
* **Priority:** P1 — Critical / Deep
* **Category:** CAPITAL_MARKETS
* **Institution:** SEBI
* **Event Date:** 2026-06-18
* **Revision Effort:** ~8 min
* **Status:** NOTIFIED

#### What Happened
* SEBI overhauled the trading framework for commodity Exchange Traded Funds (ETFs), introducing dynamic price bands, objective base-price calculation methodologies, and a pre-open call auction mechanism to eliminate pricing anomalies and align secondary ETF market trading with underlying physical and commodity futures prices.

#### Must Memorize
* **Dynamic Price Bands Architecture:**
  * For equity and debt ETFs (excluding overnight and liquid ETFs), SEBI instituted **dynamic price bands with an initial limit of $\pm 10\%$**.
  * The price band can be expanded up to **$\pm 20\%$** following a mandatory cooling-off period:
    * **15-minute cooling-off period** if the ETF price moves $9.90\%$ or more from the base price during normal trading.
    * **5-minute cooling-off period** if such extreme movement occurs during the **final 30 minutes** of trading.
  * Overnight and liquid ETFs continue to operate under a **fixed price band of $\pm 5\%$**.
* **Base Price Calculation Hierarchy:**
  1. Base price is determined as the **Volume-Weighted Average Price (VWAP)** of trades executed during the **last 30 minutes of trading** on the previous trading day.
  2. If no trades occur in the last 30 minutes $\longrightarrow$ **Last Traded Price (LTP)** of the previous trading day is used.
  3. If no trades occur throughout the entire trading day $\longrightarrow$ Latest available **closing Net Asset Value (NAV)** serves as base price.
  4. Exchanges and AMCs must transition to using **$T-1$ day closing NAV** as the universal base price from **April 1, 2027**.
* **Effective Date:** **September 1, 2026**.

#### Know / Understand
* Protects retail investors from execution slippage in illiquid commodity ETFs by capping intraday swings with cooling-off triggers and anchoring prices to 30-minute VWAP.

#### Exam Focus
* Initial dynamic price band for ETFs: $\pm 10\%$ (expandable to $\pm 20\%$).
* Fixed price band for overnight/liquid ETFs: $\pm 5\%$.
* Cooling-off duration: 15 minutes (normal) vs 5 minutes (last 30 mins).
* Base price fallback: 30-min VWAP $\to$ LTP $\to$ Closing NAV $\to$ T-1 NAV (by April 2027).

### 2. RBI Master Directions: Payment System Operators (PSO) Perpetual Licensing & Governance
* **Priority:** P1 — Critical / Memorize
* **Category:** DIGITAL_PAYMENTS
* **Institution:** RBI
* **Event Date:** 2026-06-20
* **Revision Effort:** ~7 min
* **Status:** NOTIFIED

#### What Happened
* The Reserve Bank of India (RBI) issued comprehensive Master Directions on Authorisation to Operate a Payment System, granting perpetual operational validity to compliant Payment System Operators (PSOs) while imposing strict cross-border investment caps on FATF non-compliant jurisdictions.

#### Must Memorize
* **Perpetual Licensing Norms:**
  * Authorisations granted to new PSOs will be **perpetually valid** (removing periodic renewal bureaucracy).
  * Existing operators receive perpetual validity upon renewal provided they meet regulatory criteria with no supervisory concerns; non-compliant operators receive only **1-year temporary renewals**.
* **FATF Non-Compliant Jurisdiction Safeguards:**
  * Investors originating from jurisdictions identified by the Financial Action Task Force (FATF) as non-compliant with AML/CFT standards **cannot acquire significant influence in a PSO**.
  * Aggregate voting rights and shareholding for such investors are **strictly capped below 20%**.
* **Voluntary Surrender & Cooling-off:**
  * Mandates formalized exit, consumer escrow settlement, and cooling-off frameworks for entities voluntarily surrendering PSO authorisations.

#### Exam Focus
* License validity: Perpetual for new compliant PSOs (1-year conditional for deficient operators).
* FATF non-compliant jurisdiction investment cap: Aggregate voting rights strictly below 20%.
