### 1. PFRDA Digital Architecture: NPS PRIDE-Disha & Pension Sahayak Redressal
* **Priority:** P1 — Critical / Deep
* **Category:** PENSION_SYSTEMS
* **Institution:** PFRDA
* **Event Date:** 2026-07-12
* **Revision Effort:** ~7 min
* **Status:** IMPLEMENTED

#### What Happened
* The Pension Fund Regulatory and Development Authority (PFRDA) rolled out two major digital platforms—**NPS PRIDE-Disha** and **Pension Sahayak**—to modernize fund performance benchmarking and institutionalize a structured, multi-tier grievance escalation mechanism for National Pension System (NPS), Unified Pension Scheme (UPS), and Atal Pension Yojana (APY) subscribers.

#### Must Memorize
* **NPS PRIDE-Disha (Performance Decision Support):**
  * Uses the **Extended Internal Rate of Return (XIRR)** methodology to benchmark historical pension fund performance against actual periodic subscriber contribution cashflows (replacing point-to-point CAGR which misrepresents regular monthly deposits).
* **Pension Sahayak (AI Grievance Management System):**
  * Replaces the legacy grievance platform.
  * **Strict Two-Tier Escalation Timeline:**
    * **Tier 1 (Intermediary):** Point of Presence (PoP) or Central Recordkeeping Agency (CRA) must resolve the subscriber complaint within **30 days**.
    * **Tier 2 (NPS Trust Escalation):** If unresolved or dissatisfied after 30 days, escalated to NPS Trust, which must issue a formal response within **21 days**.
  * Integrated with the Government's **Bhashini platform** to offer multi-lingual text and voice assistance across **22 Indian languages**.

#### Know / Understand
* Moves pension evaluation from static point-to-point percentage gains to actual investor cashflow returns (XIRR), and enforces binding SLAs on pension intermediaries through AI-driven escalation.

#### Exam Focus
* PRIDE-Disha methodology: Extended Internal Rate of Return (XIRR).
* Pension Sahayak resolution timelines: 30 days for Intermediaries $\to$ 21 days for NPS Trust.
* Language coverage: 22 languages via Bhashini.

### 2. SEBI Capital Market Investor Protection: Quick Transmission Processing (QTP) & Buyback ISIN Freeze
* **Priority:** P1 — Critical / Memorize
* **Category:** CAPITAL_MARKETS
* **Institution:** SEBI
* **Event Date:** 2026-07-18
* **Revision Effort:** ~7 min
* **Status:** NOTIFIED

#### What Happened
* SEBI notified structural investor-protection norms streamlining the transmission of securities for deceased investor legal heirs through **Quick Transmission Processing (QTP)**, while closing market-manipulation loopholes by enforcing an automated **ISIN-level freeze on promoter shareholdings** during share buybacks.

#### Must Memorize
* **Quick Transmission Processing (QTP) Thresholds:**
  * Fast-track transmission under simplified documentation applies to claims involving:
    * **Physical Securities:** Value up to **₹10,000**.
    * **Demat Holdings:** Value up to **₹30,000**.
* **ISIN-Level Promoter Buyback Freeze:**
  * Promoter and promoter group holdings (including associates) are **frozen at the ISIN level in depositories** from the date the Board of Directors or shareholders approve the buyback until the offer officially closes.
  * Prevents insider trading and front-running during the sensitive buyback execution window.
* **SCORES 2.0 Redressal SLA:**
  * Regulated entities and listed companies must submit a binding **Action Taken Report (ATR) within 21 days** of receiving an investor grievance on SCORES.

#### Exam Focus
* QTP monetary limits: ₹10,000 (Physical) vs ₹30,000 (Demat).
* Promoter buyback freeze: Applies from Board/Shareholder approval date until offer closure.
* SCORES 2.0 ATR timeline: 21 days.

### 1. SEBI (Employees' Service) Amendment Regulations 2026: Conflict-of-Interest Firewall
* **Priority:** P2 — High
* **Category:** REGULATORY_BODIES | **Institution:** SEBI | **Event Date:** 2026-07-08
* **Must Memorize Facts:**
  * Employees barred from investing directly in equities, equity-convertible instruments, and derivatives.
  * Investment exposure to regulated financial products capped at **25% of an employee's total investments**.
  * Mandates a **2-year cooling-off period** before former employees can represent clients before SEBI in proceedings or settlements.
  * Gift disclosure threshold raised from ₹10,000 to **₹50,000**; job negotiations must be disclosed within **1 month**.

* **Priority:** P2 — High
* **Category:** BANKING_OPERATIONS | **Institution:** RBI / Govt of India | **Event Date:** 2026-07-14
