### 1. RBI Revamps Integrated Ombudsman Scheme: Mandatory 30-Day Turnaround & ₹30 Lakh Compensation Cap
* **Priority:** P1 — Critical / Deep
* **Category:** BANKING_REGULATION
* **Institution:** RBI
* **Event Date:** 2026-07-01
* **Revision Effort:** ~10 min
* **Status:** IMPLEMENTED

#### What Happened
* The Reserve Bank of India's (RBI) revamped **Integrated Ombudsman Scheme** came into effect on **July 1, 2026**, replacing the erstwhile 2021 framework to expedite customer grievance redressal and establish strict institutional liability.
* Introduces a mandatory sequential escalation process: customers must first approach the concerned regulated entity (bank, NBFC, payment system participant).
* If the entity fails to respond within **30 days** (or within specific timelines set by RBI/NPCI/card networks) or provides an unsatisfactory resolution, the customer can escalate the complaint to the RBI Ombudsman within **90 days**.

#### Must Memorize
* **Effective Date:** **July 1, 2026** (supersedes the 2021 Integrated Ombudsman framework).
* **Escalation Timeline:**
  * **Entity Resolution Window:** **30 calendar days** for the regulated entity to respond.
  * **Ombudsman Escalation Window:** Complaint must be filed with the RBI Ombudsman within **90 days** post the 30-day window or rejection.
* **Statutory Compensation Ceilings:**
  * **Consequential Financial Loss:** Up to **₹30 lakh** (reimbursing actual financial loss arising directly from the act/omission).
  * **Harassment, Mental Anguish & Loss of Time:** Up to **₹3 lakh** (awarded to the complainant for mental distress, expenses, and harassment).
* **Grievance Trend Benchmark:** In FY25, **Chandigarh** recorded the highest RBI Ombudsman complaint rate (over 4.5 times the national average), with **loans and advances** forming the single largest complaint category.

#### Know / Understand
* Prevents premature bypass of internal dispute resolution while enforcing significant financial deterrents against sluggish customer service. Replaces fragmented ombudsman schemes with single-window digital escalation under the Centralised Receipt and Processing Centre (CRPC).

#### Exam Focus
* Exact compensation limits: ₹30 lakh (consequential loss) and ₹3 lakh (mental anguish/harassment).
* Timelines: 30 days for bank response $\to$ 90 days window for escalating to RBI Ombudsman.
* Effective date: July 1, 2026.

### 2. Central Government Notifies Employees’ Provident Fund Scheme 2026 & 3-Day Settlement Rule
* **Priority:** P1 — Critical / Deep
* **Category:** BANKING_REGULATION
* **Institution:** EPFO
* **Event Date:** 2026-07-01
* **Revision Effort:** ~10 min
* **Status:** NOTIFIED

#### What Happened
* The Central Government officially notified the **Employees’ Provident Funds Scheme, 2026**, comprehensively replacing the archaic **Employees’ Provident Funds Scheme, 1952**.
* Concurrently overhauled EPFO operating rules to mandate **3-day digital settlement** for PF withdrawal claims, backed by a **12% penal interest** on official delays beyond 20 days.
* Introduced the concept of **'Principal Employer'** for contract workers, making the principal employer directly liable for contributions if contractors are not independently registered.

#### Must Memorize
* **Statutory Scheme Replacement:** EPF Scheme 2026 replaces EPF Scheme 1952.
* **Contribution & Remittance Norms:**
  * **Remittance Deadline:** Employers must remit both employer and employee contributions (plus admin charges) within **15 days of the close of every month**.
  * **Contribution Rates:** Retained at **12%** standard (and **10%** for specifically notified establishments).
  * **Wage Ceiling Linkage:** Mandatory contributions remain explicitly linked to the statutory wage ceiling of **₹15,000 per month**.
* **Streamlined Withdrawal Architecture:**
  * Withdrawal categories compressed from **13 complex heads into 3 streamlined categories**:
    1. **Essential Needs** (Illness of self/family, education, marriage).
    2. **Housing Needs**.
    3. **Special Circumstances**.
  * **Mandatory Minimum Balance:** **25%** of the total PF corpus must be preserved at all times during partial withdrawals.
  * **Illness Withdrawal:** Members can withdraw up to **100% of eligible balance** (which equals **75% of total accumulated funds** after retaining the 25% minimum balance) after completing **12 months of total membership**.
  * **Unemployment Full Withdrawal:** **100% full corpus withdrawal** permitted after **1 year of continuous unemployment**.
  * **Superannuation / Retirement Age:** The age threshold for full withdrawal of the PF corpus upon superannuation lowered to **55 years** (from 58 years; 58 years continues for international workers under social security agreements).
* **EPFO 3-Day Settlement Rule & Penal Interest:**
  * **PF Withdrawal Settlement:** Mandated within **3 days**.
  * **Pension & EDLI Claims:** Mandated within **20 days**.
  * **Penal Interest on Official Delay:** **12% per annum** penal interest imposed on officials for delays exceeding 20 days.
* **EPFO Infrastructure Upgrades:**
  * Rolled out **CITES 2.01** (Centralised IT Enabled Services) migrating 340 million accounts across 120 decentralised databases into a single unified national architecture.
  * Auto-settlement limit for advance claims raised from **₹1 lakh to ₹5 lakh** for KYC-compliant members.
  * Interest rate for EPF subscribers maintained at **8.25% for FY26** (credited by July 15, months ahead of schedule).

#### Know / Understand
* Radical transformation of India's largest retirement fund from a slow, discretionary bureaucracy into an automated, algorithmic platform. The 25% minimum balance rule prevents premature depletion of retirement funds while giving liquidity for major life emergencies.

#### Exam Focus
* Scheme year (EPF Scheme 2026 replacing 1952 Scheme).
* Withdrawal categories (3 heads: Essential, Housing, Special).
* Minimum mandatory balance requirement (25%).
* Full withdrawal age threshold (55 years).
* Settlement timelines (3 days for PF; 20 days for Pension/EDLI; 12% penal interest).
* CITES 2.01 auto-settlement advance limit (₹5 lakh).

### 3. RBI Final Directions: Non-Recognition of Unrealised Interest on Specified Non-Financial Assets (SNFAs)
* **Priority:** P1 — Critical / Deep
* **Category:** BANKING_REGULATION
* **Institution:** RBI
* **Event Date:** 2026-07-08
* **Revision Effort:** ~10 min
* **Status:** NOTIFIED

#### What Happened
* The Reserve Bank of India issued the **Reserve Bank of India (Commercial Banks – Resolution of Stressed Assets) Third Amendment Directions, 2026**, directing all Commercial Banks, Small Finance Banks (SFBs), and NBFCs **not to recognise unrealised interest and charges as income** when acquiring Specified Non-Financial Assets (SNFAs) in debt resolution.
* Prohibits artificial income inflation by preventing lenders from booking unpaid accrued interest into Profit & Loss accounts upon acquiring stressed collateral.

#### Must Memorize
* **Definition of SNFA (Paragraph 6(15A)):** An immovable asset acquired by a bank in satisfaction or part satisfaction of its claims on a borrower, including Non-Banking Assets (NBAs) acquired under the Banking Regulation Act, 1949.
* **Effective Date:** Comes into force from **October 1, 2026**.
* **P&L Reversal Mandate:** Any unrealised income on SNFAs outstanding on bank books as of **September 30, 2026**, must be completely reversed through the Profit and Loss account by **September 30, 2027**, to the extent it remains unrealised.
* **Disposal & Auction Rules (Paragraphs 13B & 140J–140M):**
  * **Mandatory Disposal Period:** Banks must dispose of acquired SNFAs within a maximum period not exceeding **7 years**.
  * **Public Auction Requirement:** Disposal must occur via public auction adhering strictly to the auction principles of the **SARFAESI Act, 2002**.
  * **Strict Related-Party Bar:** SNFAs **shall NOT be sold back to the borrower or its related parties** (as defined under the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code, 2016), even if the asset ceases to be an SNFA.
  * **Own-Use Reclassification:** If an SNFA is put to the bank's own operational use, it ceases to be an SNFA and must be recorded under **'Fixed Assets'**.

#### Know / Understand
* Resolves an accounting loophole where banks booked non-cash profits upon repossessing stressed properties. Forces honest asset valuation, rapid collateral liquidations under SARFAESI within 7 years, and stops circular asset buybacks by defaulting promoters.

#### Exam Focus
* Effective date: October 1, 2026; Reversal deadline: September 30, 2027.
* Maximum disposal period for SNFAs: 7 years.
* Prohibition of sale back to defaulting borrowers/related parties under IBC 2016 definition.
* Legal framework: Section 35A of BR Act 1949 and SARFAESI Act 2002.

### 4. RBI Financial Inclusion Index (FI-Index) March 2026: Rises to 70.0
* **Priority:** P1 — Critical / Memorize
* **Category:** BANKING_REGULATION
* **Institution:** RBI
* **Event Date:** 2026-07-09
* **Revision Effort:** ~6 min
* **Status:** NOTIFIED

#### What Happened
* The Reserve Bank of India published the annual **Financial Inclusion Index (FI-Index)** for the year ending **March 2026**, showing deepening financial penetration and quality across India.
* The composite index rose to **70.0 in March 2026** (from **67.0 in March 2025**), registering an annual growth of **4.48%**.

#### Must Memorize
* **Index Value:** **70.0** (March 2026) vs **67.0** (March 2025); Annual growth: **4.48%**.
* **Index Architecture & Design:**
  * **Base Year:** **No base year** (continuous dynamic tracking).
  * **Scale:** Single composite metric ranging from **0 to 100** (0 = Complete exclusion, 100 = Full inclusion).
  * **Total Indicators:** Tracks **97 indicators** across banking, investments, insurance, postal, and pension sectors.
* **Three Core Sub-Indices & Weights:**
  1. **Access:** **35% weight** (availability of physical/digital banking touchpoints, ATMs, branches, BCs).
  2. **Usage:** **45% weight** (active deposit, credit, remittance, digital transaction volumes).
  3. **Quality:** **20% weight** (financial literacy, consumer protection, grievances, inequalities).
* **Inception History:** First introduced in **August 2021** (for FY ended March 2021) and published annually in July.

#### Know / Understand
* The 'Usage' dimension holds the highest weight (45%) because opening bank accounts (Access) is ineffective unless accounts see active, sustained financial throughput.

#### Exam Focus
* Latest FI-Index score: **70.0** (March 2026).
* Growth rate: **4.48%**.
* Weights of three parameters: Access (35%), Usage (45%), Quality (20%).
* Number of indicators: 97; Base year: None.

#### What Happened
* The Pension Fund Regulatory and Development Authority (PFRDA) invited applications from prospective financial institutions to manage the retirement corpus under the National Pension System (NPS) by opening an ongoing **'on-tap' registration window**.
* Replaces periodic bidding with continuous open entry to foster institutional competition and lower management costs.

#### Must Memorize
* **Mandatory Eligibility Criteria for New PFMs:**
  * **Experience Requirement:** Minimum **5 years of experience** in managing equity and debt investment portfolios.
  * **Regulatory Registration:** Applicant must be registered and regulated by at least one primary financial regulator: **RBI, SEBI, or IRDAI**.
  * **Net Worth Requirement:** Minimum net worth of **₹50 crore** maintained across each of the **last 5 financial years**.
  * **Paid-Up Equity Capital:** Minimum paid-up capital of **₹25 crore** at the time of application.

  * **ASCEND Committee:** Set up a high-level committee named **ASCEND** (*Accelerated Scaling of Global Capital Ecosystem and NPS Development*) chaired by **Dinesh Khara** (Chairman, NPS Trust) to create a roadmap for channeling global long-term pension capital into India.
  * **PRIDE-Disha Tool:** Launched a digital decision-support tool using **Extended Internal Rate of Return (XIRR)** over 5,000 days of historical NAV data to evaluate actual PFM returns.
  * **Pension Sahayak:** AI-powered grievance platform for NPS, UPS, and APY (Intermediary resolution: 30 days; NPS Trust escalation: 21 days; support in 22 languages via Bhashini).

#### Know / Understand
* Democratizes the management of India's fast-growing retirement savings pool by allowing qualified domestic and foreign joint-venture asset managers meeting the ₹50 crore net worth threshold to manage NPS assets on tap.

#### Exam Focus
* Net worth requirement for PFM: ₹50 crore (for 5 consecutive FYs).
* Paid-up capital requirement: ₹25 crore.
* Minimum investment management track record: 5 years.

### 6. RBI Master Circular on Special Rupee Vostro Accounts (SRVAs) & Cross-Border INR Trade
* **Priority:** P1 — Critical / Deep
* **Category:** BANKING_REGULATION
* **Institution:** RBI
* **Event Date:** 2026-07-05
* **Revision Effort:** ~8 min
* **Status:** NOTIFIED

#### What Happened
* The Reserve Bank of India issued a consolidated Master Circular rationalising instructions across five previous circulars issued between July 2022 and October 2025 regarding **Special Rupee Vostro Accounts (SRVAs)**.
* Standardized cross-border trade invoicing, payment, and settlement in Indian Rupees (INR) under the Foreign Exchange Management (Deposit) Regulations, 2016.

#### Must Memorize
* **Permitted Account Architecture:**
  * Authorised Dealer Category-I (AD-I) banks in India can open SRVAs for their overseas branches or correspondent banks resident outside India without prior RBI approval (subject to reporting).
  * **Dedicated Exporter/Importer Accounts:** AD banks maintaining SRVAs are permitted to open an **additional current account** for domestic exporters and importers exclusively for the settlement of rupee-denominated trade transactions.
* **Transparency & Directory Mandate:** AD banks must periodically update details of all operational SRVAs in the centralized **SRVA Directory** published and maintained by the **Foreign Exchange Dealers' Association of India (FEDAI)**.
* **Eligible Transactions:** Direct invoicing, payment, and settlement for all permissible current account and capital account transactions under FEMA. Surplus INR balances in SRVAs can be invested in Indian Government Securities (G-Secs, T-Bills).
* **EbixCash Milestone:** EbixCash World Money became the **first Authorised Dealer Category-II (AD-II) entity** to receive a perpetual licence from RBI under revised FEMA rules, with trade remittances permitted up to **₹25 lakh per transaction**.

#### Know / Understand
* Eliminates exchange rate risk for Indian exporters/importers by establishing INR as a direct international invoicing currency, reducing dependency on US Dollar clearing channels and deepening foreign central bank investments in Indian sovereign debt.

#### Exam Focus
* Permitted operational mechanism for SRVAs under FEMA (Deposit) Regulations 2016.
* Entity maintaining the national SRVA directory: FEDAI.
* First AD-II entity to receive perpetual RBI licence: EbixCash (₹25 lakh trade remittance limit).

### 7. SEBI Revamps FPI Regulations: Rupee-Denominated Fees & Intraday Mutual Fund Borrowing
* **Priority:** P1 — Critical / Deep
* **Category:** FINANCIAL_MARKETS
* **Institution:** SEBI
* **Event Date:** 2026-07-07
* **Revision Effort:** ~8 min
* **Status:** NOTIFIED

#### What Happened
* The Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) notified landmark amendments to the **Foreign Portfolio Investors (FPI) Regulations** and **Mutual Fund Regulations**.
* Replaced US Dollar-denominated fee structures with a Rupee-denominated regime for FPIs and Foreign Venture Capital Investors (FVCIs).
* Permitted mutual funds to execute **intraday borrowing** to eliminate settlement timing mismatches.

#### Must Memorize
* **Rupee-Denominated FPI Fee Restructuring (Regulation 43B):**
  * Substituted US $1,000 application fee with **₹90,000** in eligible forex equivalent.
  * **Category-I FPI & FVCI Registration Charge:** Revised from $2,500 to **₹2.3 lakh**.
  * **Custodian Fee Structure:** Shifted from an annual payment of ₹10 lakh to a monthly payment of **₹85,000 per month**.
  * **DDP Remittance Timeline:** Designated Depository Participants (DDPs) must remit collected fees to SEBI within **5 working days** of granting registration.
  * **Common Application Form (CAF) Update:** Mandatory inclusion of date of birth/incorporation to streamline automatic PAN allocation under CBDT rules.
* **Mutual Funds Intraday Borrowing Framework:**
  * Mutual funds permitted to borrow funds intraday to bridge temporary differences arising from pay-in and pay-out settlement cycles across asset classes and forex transactions.
  * **In Addition to Existing Limit:** Operates over and above the existing borrowing limit of **up to 20% of net assets** of a scheme allowed for meeting unit-holder redemptions (which carries a maximum 6-month tenure).
* **Additional SEBI Key Directives in July 2026:**
  * **Investor Protection Fund (IPF):** Depositories allowed to use up to **5% of annual income** from IPF for administrative and employee expenses; **at least 95%** must be ploughed back into the corpus.
  * **Mutual Fund-Only PMS:** Proposed new PMS category with minimum client investment reduced to **₹25 lakh** (vs ₹50 lakh standard) and applicant net worth reduced to **₹2 crore** (vs ₹5 crore); fixed management fee capped at **2.5% of AUM**.
  * **Security Transmission (QTP):** Quick Transmission Processing for claims up to **₹10,000 (physical)** and **₹30,000 (demat)**; simplified documentation threshold up to **₹10 lakh (physical)** and **₹30 lakh (demat)**; requests processed within **21 calendar days**.

#### Know / Understand
* Eliminates operational frictions in multi-market T+1 settlement cycles for mutual funds while de-dollarizing regulatory fees in Indian capital markets.

#### Exam Focus
* Revised Cat-I FPI registration fee: ₹2.3 lakh (replacing $2,500); Application fee: ₹90,000 (replacing $1,000).
* Custodian fee: ₹85,000/month.
* MF intraday borrowing permission (over and above the 20% redemption borrowing limit).
* Depository IPF expense cap: 5% maximum (95% ploughed back).
* MF-only PMS thresholds: ₹25 lakh ticket size, ₹2 crore net worth, 2.5% fee cap.
