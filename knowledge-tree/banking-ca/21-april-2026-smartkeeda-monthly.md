### 1. RBI Increases Borrowing Limits Against Securities: ₹1 Crore for Shares & ₹25 Lakh for IPOs
* **Priority:** P1 — Critical / Deep
* **Category:** BANKING_REGULATION
* **Institution:** RBI
* **Event Date:** 2026-04-03
* **Revision Effort:** ~8 min
* **Status:** NOTIFIED

#### What Happened
* The Reserve Bank of India revised the regulatory framework for individual borrowing against securities, substantially enhancing borrowing ceilings to facilitate orderly capital market participation while instituting system-wide risk limits.
* **Implementation Deadline:** The implementation date for the revised capital market exposure framework has been deferred to **July 1, 2026**.

#### Must Memorize
* **Enhanced Individual Borrowing Ceilings:**
  * **Loans Against Shares & Eligible Securities (REITs/InvITs):** Raised from ₹20 lakh to **₹1 crore per borrower**.
  * **IPO, FPO, and ESOP Subscriptions:** Raised from ₹10 lakh to **₹25 lakh per individual**.
* **System-Wide Aggregate Cap:**
  * Borrowing limits now apply as an **aggregate system-wide cap across the entire banking sector**, rather than per individual lending institution, preventing borrowers from bypassing exposure limits by obtaining multiple loans across different banks.
* **Removal of Regulatory Ceiling on Listed Debt:**
  * The regulatory ceiling on bank lending against **listed debt securities** has been completely removed.
* **Standard Margin Requirements:**
  * **Physical Shares:** Minimum margin of **50%**.
  * **Dematerialised (Demat) Shares:** Minimum margin of **25%**.

#### Know / Understand
* Previously, investors could take ₹20 lakh from multiple banks simultaneously to finance speculative share purchases. By converting the cap into a PAN-linked system-wide limit of ₹1 crore (and ₹25 lakh for IPOs), RBI balances retail liquidity access with macroprudential safety.

#### Exam Focus
* Loans against shares ceiling: ₹1 crore per borrower (earlier ₹20 lakh).
* IPO/FPO/ESOP financing limit: ₹25 lakh per individual (earlier ₹10 lakh).
* System-wide cap rule preventing multi-bank borrowing.
* Margin requirements: 50% for physical shares and 25% for demat shares.
* Implementation effective date: July 1, 2026.

### 2. NSO MoSPI: Periodic Labour Force Survey (PLFS) Annual Report (Jan–Dec 2025)
* **Priority:** P1 — Critical / Deep
* **Category:** REPORTS_AND_INDICES
* **Institution:** MOSPI
* **Event Date:** 2026-04-15
* **Revision Effort:** ~8 min
* **Status:** NOTIFIED

#### What Happened
* The National Statistical Office (NSO) under the Ministry of Statistics and Programme Implementation (MoSPI) released the **Periodic Labour Force Survey (PLFS) Annual Report (January–December 2025)**.
* **Structural Transition:** This marks the **first PLFS annual report following the calendar year** (January–December 2025) rather than the historical agricultural year (July–June).

#### Must Memorize
* **All-India Labour Market Indicators (Age 15+ Usual Status):**
  * **Unemployment Rate (UR):** Stood at **3.1%** overall (Rural: **2.4%** [Male 2.6%, Female 2.1%]; Urban: **4.8%** [Male 4.2%, Female 6.4%]).
  * **Labour Force Participation Rate (LFPR - CWS):** **59.3%** overall (Male: **79.1%**, Female: **40.0%**).
  * **Worker Population Ratio (WPR):** **57.4%** overall (Male: **76.6%**, Female: **38.8%**).
  * **Youth Unemployment Rate (Age 15–29 Years):** Declined to **9.9%**.
* **Employment Structure Breakdown:**
  * **Self-Employed:** **56.2%** of the workforce.
  * **Regular Wage / Salaried Employees:** **23.6%** (increased).
  * **Agriculture Sector Employment:** Dropped to **43.0%**.
  * **Manufacturing Sector Employment:** Rose to **12.1%**.
* **Core Statistical Definitions:**
  * **LFPR:** Percentage of persons in the labour force (working, seeking, or available for work) in the total population.
  * **WPR:** Percentage of employed persons in the total population.
  * **UR:** Percentage of unemployed persons among the total labour force.
  * **Current Weekly Status (CWS):** Activity status determined based on the 7 days preceding the survey.

#### Know / Understand
* Shifting PLFS from the agricultural cycle (July–June) to the calendar year synchronizes employment indicators with corporate fiscal reporting and the newly revised national accounts base year (2022–23).
