### 1. Taxation and Other Laws (Amendment) Bill, 2026: Enabling Provision for UPI & Digital Payment Charges
- **Category**: `BANKING_REGULATION`
- **Institution**: `GOVERNMENT_OF_INDIA`
- **Priority**: `P1_CRITICAL_DEEP`
- **Date**: `2026-08-20`
- **Target Exams**: `SBI PO Mains, IBPS PO Mains`
- **Revision Effort**: `~8 min`
- **Source**: `Smartkeeda (Aug W3 2026)`

#### Legislative Overview & Core Provision
The Lok Sabha passed the **Taxation and Other Laws (Amendment) Bill, 2026** by voice vote, introducing an enabling legal provision that empowers the Central Government to permit banks and payment service providers to levy charges on Unified Payments Interface (UPI) and other notified electronic payment modes.

#### Context & Rationale
- **MDR Legal Restriction Overhaul**: The amendment reworks **Section 10A of the Payment and Settlement Systems Act, 2007**, which previously barred banks and payment providers from imposing any Merchant Discount Rate (MDR) or transaction charge on payment modes covered under Section 269SU of the Income Tax Act (including RuPay debit cards and BHIM-UPI QR codes).
- **Enabling Nature (No Immediate Levy)**: The amendment does **not immediately impose charges** on UPI transactions; it creates an enabling statutory framework allowing the Government to notify specific payment modes, fee caps, and merchant categories in the future.

#### Key Structural Provisions & Digital Payment Metrics
- **Amended Acts**: Formally amends the **Payment and Settlement Systems Act, 2007**, the **Income Tax Act, 2025**, and the **Finance Act, 2026**.
- **Section 269SU Background**: Businesses with an annual turnover exceeding **₹50 crore** are statutorily required to accept payments through specified digital modes.
- **Comparative Payment Landscape**: While RTGS and NEFT have long attracted service charges, UPI has remained zero-fee for both consumers and merchants since inception.
- **UPI Scale Milestone (July 2026)**: UPI recorded **23.66 billion transactions** valued at **₹29.88 lakh crore** in July 2026.

#### Key Exam Takeaways
- **Amended Section**: Section 10A of the Payment and Settlement Systems Act, 2007.
- **Associated Income Tax Provision**: Section 269SU (mandatory digital payment acceptance for businesses >₹50 cr turnover).
- **Immediate Effect**: Enabling legal provision only; zero immediate fee until explicitly notified.
- **Monthly Record (July 2026)**: 23.66 billion transactions worth ₹29.88 lakh crore.

### 2. CBDT FAST-DS 2026: Foreign Assets of Small Taxpayers-Disclosure Scheme
- **Category**: `BANKING_REGULATION`
- **Institution**: `CBDT`
- **Priority**: `P1_CRITICAL_MEMORIZE`
- **Date**: `2026-08-16`
- **Target Exams**: `SBI PO Mains, IBPS PO Mains`
- **Revision Effort**: `~7 min`
- **Source**: `Smartkeeda (Aug W3 2026)`

#### Scheme Overview & Window
The Central Board of Direct Taxes (CBDT) operationalised the **Foreign Assets of Small Taxpayers-Disclosure Scheme (FAST-DS), 2026**, notified under **Chapter IV (Sections 130–144) of the Finance Act, 2026**, opening a one-time compliance window from **August 16, 2026 to December 31, 2026** for eligible small taxpayers to declare undisclosed foreign income and assets.

#### Statutory Mechanism & Valuation Rules
- **4 Eligible Asset Categories**:
  1. Undisclosed assets located outside India (including financial interests in foreign entities).
  2. Undisclosed foreign income chargeable to tax in India.
  3. Assets acquired abroad during non-residence periods but not declared upon becoming Indian residents.
  4. Assets purchased from taxed income but inadvertently omitted from the foreign asset schedule of ITR.
- **Valuation Cut-Off Date**: Fixed at **March 31, 2026** (Fair market value computed as higher of acquisition cost and open market price).
- **Foreign Bank Accounts Computation**: Value computed as the sum of all deposits made from account opening date up to March 31, 2026 (excluding double-counted redeposited withdrawals).
- **Procedural Timeline**: Declarations filed electronically in **Form 1** by December 31, 2026; Tax authorities issue determination order in **Form 2**; Declarant must pay determined tax/fee within **2 months without interest**.

#### Key Exam Takeaways
- **Statutory Authority**: Chapter IV (Sections 130–144) of the Finance Act, 2026.
- **Operational Window**: August 16, 2026 to December 31, 2026.
- **Valuation Date**: March 31, 2026.
- **Procedural Forms**: Form 1 (Declaration) $\to$ Form 2 (Determination Order).
- **Payment Horizon**: 2 months from Form 2 order without interest.

### 3. PM E-DRIVE Scheme Extended to March 2028: Outlay Raised to ₹11,900 Crore

#### Scheme Revision & Outlay Expansion
The Ministry of Heavy Industries (MHI) amended the **PM Electric Drive Revolution in Innovative Vehicle Enhancement (PM E-DRIVE)** scheme, extending demand incentives for electric two-wheelers (e-2Ws) until **March 31, 2028** and raising the overall scheme outlay to **₹11,900 crore** (up from ₹10,900 crore).

#### Revised Incentive Architecture & Fiscal Sub-Limits
- **E-2W Segment Enhancement**: Sanctioned an additional **₹1,000 crore** exclusively for electric two-wheelers, increasing total e-2W allocation from ₹1,772 crore to **₹2,767 crore** and targeting **45.79 lakh e-2Ws**.
- **Tapered Incentive Structure**: Demand incentive for e-2Ws reduced to **₹2,500 per kWh, capped at ₹5,000 per vehicle** (down from earlier ₹5,000/kWh capped at ₹10,000/vehicle).
- **Price & Proportion Cap**: Electric two-wheelers with ex-showroom prices up to **₹1.50 lakh** remain eligible, capped at **15% of ex-factory price** (whichever is lower).
- **Claim Submission Deadline**: Last date for submitting e-2W incentive claims is **December 31, 2027** (zero payments permitted after March 31, 2028).
- **Electric Ambulances**: Allocation of **₹500 crore** supporting **3,811 electric ambulances** across FY27 and FY28.
- **Predecessor Frameworks**: PM E-DRIVE formally replaced **FAME-I, FAME-II, and EMPS 2024**.

#### Key Exam Takeaways
- **Total Scheme Outlay**: ₹11,900 crore (extended from April 1, 2024 to March 31, 2028).
- **E-2W Segment Outlay**: ₹2,767 crore (targeting 45.79 lakh electric 2-wheelers).
- **Revised Incentive Rate**: ₹2,500/kWh capped at ₹5,000/vehicle (max 15% ex-factory price).
- **Claim Cut-off**: December 31, 2027.

- **Revision Effort**: `~7 min`

#### Policy Announcement & Historical Significance
The Office of the Registrar General of India (RGI), Ministry of Home Affairs, notified a **40-question Household Schedule** for Phase 2 of Census 2027, marking the **first comprehensive caste enumeration in Independent India** and the first since the **1931 British Census**.

#### Key Structural Elements & Digital Infrastructure
- **Caste Enumeration Entry**: Question 10 is an open-ended entry titled *"Scheduled Caste (SC)/Scheduled Tribe (ST)/Caste"* applicable to all citizens.
- **Financial & Identity Data**: For the first time in Census history, collects document numbers (Aadhaar, Voter ID, Passport, Driving Licence, Mobile) and the **total number of bank accounts held**.
- **Two-Phase Architecture**:
  - **Phase 1**: 33-question Houselisting and Housing Census schedule (commenced **April 1, 2026**).
  - **Phase 2**: 40-question Population Enumeration & Household Schedule.
- **Legal Framework**: Governed under **Section 8(2) of the Census Act, 1948** (mandatory disclosure by citizens with statutory confidentiality).
- **100% Digital Census**: India's 1st fully digital census conducted via mobile applications and central monitoring dashboards.

#### Key Exam Takeaways
- **Question Counts**: Phase 1 = 33 questions (Houselisting) · Phase 2 = 40 questions (Household).
- **Historical Benchmark**: 1st full caste count since 1931 (96 years).
- **New Data Parameter**: Total number of bank accounts held and personal ID numbers.
- **Governing Law**: Census Act, 1948 (Section 8(2)).

### 5. CBDC-Based Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) Launched under PMGKAY
- **Category**: `BANKING_REGULATION`
- **Institution**: `RESERVE_BANK_OF_INDIA`
- **Priority**: `P1_CRITICAL_MEMORIZE`
- **Date**: `2026-08-14`
- **Target Exams**: `SBI PO Mains, IBPS PO Mains`
- **Revision Effort**: `~6 min`
- **Source**: `Smartkeeda (Aug W3 2026)`

#### Operational Mechanism & Rollout
The Government of India launched **Central Bank Digital Currency (CBDC)-based Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT)** under the **Pradhan Mantri Garib Kalyan Anna Yojana (PMGKAY)** in the Union Territories of **Chandigarh** and **Dadra & Nagar Haveli**, marking the country's first large-scale welfare deployment of the Digital Rupee.

#### Technical Architecture & Benefits
- **Programmable Digital Rupee Tokens**: PMGKAY food subsidies are transferred directly into beneficiaries' CBDC wallets as programmable tokens locked for foodgrain purchases at empanelled Fair Price Shops.
- **100% Digital Adoption**: Chandigarh and Dadra & Nagar Haveli became the first UTs in India to achieve complete CBDC-based food subsidy delivery, scaling up earlier pilot tests in Puducherry and Gujarat.
- **Direct Institutional Integration**: Eliminates intermediary bank account routing delays, offering real-time, traceable, and offline-compatible subsidy redemption.

#### Key Exam Takeaways
- **First UTs to Deploy**: Chandigarh and Dadra & Nagar Haveli.
- **Underlying Welfare Scheme**: PMGKAY (Pradhan Mantri Garib Kalyan Anna Yojana).
- **Core Technology**: Programmable CBDC (Digital Rupee) tokens.
